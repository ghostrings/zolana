A red Rings shield means registered for encrypted holdings, not private trades. The overview totals above cover the existing Meteora index only. No holder counts are shown: balances kept in Rings sit in one pooled account, and who owns what inside it is encrypted.

Four moves.

  1. Open a ghost walletA Solana keypair made in your browser and encrypted with your passphrase. Or connect your own wallet.
  2. Shield SOL into RingsA deposit is public. From then on the balance is an encrypted note only you can read.
  3. Trade from the private balanceGhost fill swaps with the desk in one co-signed transaction: asset and amount encrypted. The Shielded route trades the pool directly.
  4. Hold, send or burnTokens stay in your encrypted balance. Send them to any registered address without revealing what or how much, or burn them for good. Read the docs.

Private where it can be. Plain about the rest.

Balances are encrypted

SOL and tokens held in Rings are notes only your viewing key opens. No holder list can be read off token accounts.

Transfers hide asset and amount

A private transfer shows the two wallets and nothing else. Which token moved, and how much, is ciphertext.

Wallets are not hidden

The Default Ring is confidential, not anonymous. Sender and recipient addresses are public on every transaction.

Pool trades are public

A Meteora pool is a public account. Any swap against it shows its size. Deposits and withdrawals show theirs too.

Fixed at launch. Fixed after graduation.

A creator picks 2%, 3% or 4%. That number is written into the token's Meteora config, which cannot be changed, and the same number is written into the pool the curve graduates to. There is no schedule that lowers it and no dynamic component. On the curve, 24% of every fee goes to the creator, 56% to the platform and 20% to Meteora's protocol. After graduation the platform holds all of the locked liquidity, and the platform pays the creator 30% of the pool fees it collects.
