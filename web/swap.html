What a buy does

Either all of it happens or none of it does. Nobody holds your funds in between: your wallet signs once, for the whole thing.

  1. What you pay leaves your private balance.SOL or USDC, for exactly the amount you entered. From here on the amount is public.
  2. Jupiter swaps it for the token.On the pools of the route Jupiter chose. The instructions come from Jupiter; this page reads every one of them before your wallet sees anything, and refuses any that would pay somebody else.
  3. The token goes into your private balance.For the least the swap can return. Whatever it returns above that is moved in right afterwards, if you leave that box ticked.

The price is Jupiter’s. This page checks that the swap does what the quote says; it cannot tell you whether the quote is a good price, or whether the token can be sold again later. How this works, and what can go wrong.
