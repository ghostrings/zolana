How it works

What a run does

The same private transfer the wallet page sends, once per line. Nobody holds your funds in between: your wallet signs every payment.

  1. You paste the list and the page reads it back.One line per wallet: the address, the amount, and a note if you want one. A line it cannot read is shown to you, never guessed at and never dropped in silence.
  2. Each payment is sent and followed until the chain has decided.Its signature is written down in this browser before it is sent. If the tab closes or the connection drops, the run picks up where it stopped and no wallet is paid twice.
  3. You get a receipt.A file with every line: paid or not, the transaction, the time and your note. It is made in this browser and goes nowhere else.

A run stops by itself when a payment cannot be made, and says why. What was paid stays paid. How this works, and what an observer sees.
