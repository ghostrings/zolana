One signature

What the launch transaction does.

  1. Creates the mint and its curveA fixed supply of 1,000,000,000 tokens on a Meteora bonding curve, priced in the pair token you chose. The mint authority is revoked and the metadata cannot be edited.
  2. Opens the token to RingsIt registers the mint with Solana Privacy Rings, so anyone can hold it in an encrypted balance from the first block.
  3. Makes your first buyOptional. It is an ordinary curve trade, so its size is public.
  4. Moves it into your private balanceThe tokens you bought are deposited into Rings before the transaction ends. Your public token account is closed again.

If you chose fee modules, a second transaction follows as soon as the token is listed: it hands the token's creator role to the wallet that runs them, with your settings written on chain. How fee modules work
