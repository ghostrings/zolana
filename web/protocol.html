Exactly what is hidden. Exactly what is not.

privacycash.fun joins two public systems: Solana Privacy Rings for encrypted balances and transfers, and Meteora for bonding curves and pools. This page states what each one reveals, so nothing here has to be taken on trust. The docs go through every screen in detail.

What is private

Confidential, not anonymous.

Balances and transfer contents are encrypted. Wallet addresses are not hidden. This platform uses the Default Ring of Solana Privacy Rings, the one that is live on mainnet. It hides how much a wallet holds, and the token and amount of each private transfer. It does not hide who sent or who received.

Two consequences are worth stating plainly. A Meteora pool is a public account, so any trade that touches it shows its size, whatever happens before or after. And a token launched here is an ordinary Solana token on a public pool: anyone can also buy and hold it the ordinary, public way, from any wallet or aggregator, and those balances are as visible as any other. What is hidden is what is held in Rings. On chain that is one pooled account, and who owns what inside it is encrypted.

A burn is on the public side of that line by nature: it lowers the supply everyone reads, so the wallet, the token and the amount show. It can be made straight from a private balance, which keeps what the wallet still holds hidden. See Send privately and Burn tokens.

Trade routes

Two ways to trade from a private balance.

Ghost fill

You and the platform's desk swap two private transfers inside one transaction that you both sign. You send the desk SOL; the desk sends you tokens, or the reverse. On chain it is two encrypted transfers between two wallets: the token and the amounts are ciphertext. Either both transfers settle or neither does, so the desk never holds your funds.

Before your wallet signs, it rebuilds the desk's transfer from the data it was sent, decrypts it with your key, and checks that it pays you the quoted amount of the quoted asset. It also checks that the transaction contains exactly those two transfers and nothing else, and that the desk pays the network fee. If any check fails, nothing is signed.

What Ghost fill does not hide. The desk is your counterparty, so the desk knows your trade. An observer sees that your wallet traded with the desk. The desk restocks on the public pool later, in its own sizes and at its own times; when few people trade, that restocking can be matched to a fill by timing.

Shielded route

One atomic transaction: funds leave your private balance, trade on the Meteora curve or pool, and the result goes back into your private balance. There is no counterparty and no custody, and it works with any wallet. The pool trade in the middle is public, so your wallet, the token and the size are visible. What stays private is the balance the funds came from and the balance they return to.

A buy asks the pool for an exact number of tokens so the deposit that follows can name that number; SOL that was set aside for slippage and not used stays in your public wallet. A sell deposits the guaranteed minimum into your private balance; anything the pool paid above that arrives as public SOL.

Public entry

A buy paid with public SOL whose tokens go straight into your private balance. It is the Shielded route without the first step, for a wallet that has not deposited yet.

Pair tokens

A token is priced and traded in the pair token its creator picked at launch: SOL, or another token the operator has switched on. Everything above reads the same with that token in SOL's place. A buy spends it, a sell returns it, and fees and rewards are paid in it. Two things differ. The pair token passes through your wallet's ordinary token account for it, where anything a trade does not use stays, in public. And Ghost fill is for tokens priced in SOL only: the desk holds nothing else. Network fees are always paid in SOL. See Pair tokens.

Fees

2%, 3% or 4%. Fixed for the life of the token.

A creator picks one of three tiers at launch. The tier is a Meteora config account that cannot be edited after it is created. The fee is charged on every trade on the bonding curve, and the same percentage is written into the pool the curve graduates to.

RuleHow it is enforced
The fee never decays on the curveThe config's fee schedule has zero periods: the starting fee and the ending fee are the same number.
The fee is the same after graduationThe config sets the graduated pool's fee to the same basis points, so migration creates the pool with it.
No dynamic or volatility feeDynamic fees are disabled in the config for both phases.
The creator and the platform cannot change itA config cannot be edited once created, and the graduated pool's liquidity is locked permanently, so it cannot be moved to a cheaper pool.
This site refuses anything elseBefore every quote the app reads the pool and its config from chain and rejects a token whose fee terms differ from its tier.

On the curve, 24% of every fee goes to the creator, 56% to the platform and 20% to Meteora's protocol. After graduation the platform holds all of the locked liquidity, and the platform pays the creator 30% of the pool fees it collects. Meteora's share is set by Meteora's programs, not by this platform. On the curve the creator's share accrues on chain, and only the creator can claim it. The payment after graduation is different in kind: the platform makes it, to the creator's wallet, when it claims the pool's fees, and no contract enforces it.

Other costs: Solana's network fee, any per-transaction fee charged by Solana Privacy Rings itself, temporary account rent that is returned in the same transaction where possible, and on a Ghost fill the desk's spread, which is shown in the quote before you sign.

Launches

One signature.

A launch is a single transaction that creates the mint and its Meteora bonding curve, registers the mint with Rings so it can be held privately from the first block, and, if the creator wants, makes a first buy and deposits those tokens into the creator's private balance.

Supply is fixed at 1,000,000,000 with 6 decimals. The mint authority is revoked and the metadata cannot be edited. When the curve has raised its target, it graduates to a Meteora DAMM v2 pool and all of the pool's liquidity is locked permanently in one position, which the platform holds. That position collects the pool's fees. Nobody can withdraw the liquidity, the platform included. Graduation is a permissionless instruction that anyone can send once the curve is complete; Meteora runs a keeper that sends it for curves of this size. Trading pauses between completion and graduation.

Creating a token is public: the creator's wallet, the name, the artwork and the size of the first buy are all visible. What the creator holds afterwards is not.

Rewards

Paid into the private balance.

On the curve, a creator's share of every trading fee accrues on chain. A claim withdraws what has accrued and deposits it into the creator's private balance in the same transaction. The claim is a public event, with the wallet and the amount visible, because it is paid out of a public pool. From that point the SOL is an encrypted balance and can be sent on privately.

After graduation the creator holds no liquidity. The platform's locked position collects the pool's fees, and the platform pays the creator 30% of the pool fees it collects. That payment arrives as public SOL in the creator's wallet, in the same transaction in which the platform claims. It is the platform's payment: it happens when the platform claims, and no contract enforces it.

Keys and proofs

Self-custody, stated precisely.

The built-in ghost wallet is an ordinary Solana keypair generated in your browser. Its seed is stored encrypted with your passphrase (PBKDF2-SHA256, 310,000 rounds, then AES-256-GCM) in this browser's storage. While unlocked, the seed is kept in the tab's session storage so moving between pages does not ask again; locking, or closing the tab, removes it. You can export the key and import it into any Solana wallet. If you lose both the passphrase and the exported key, nobody can recover the wallet.

One browser can keep several ghost wallets under that one passphrase. The first is stored as described above. A wallet made in the app gets its seed from the first wallet's seed and a number (HMAC-SHA256 under a fixed label), so the first wallet's secret key brings it back. A wallet imported from another key is sealed with AES-256-GCM under a key derived from the first wallet's seed, and is covered only by its own secret key. While unlocked, the tab holds the seed of the wallet in use and the first wallet's seed, and nothing else. Names, icons and contacts are kept in the browser and are never on chain. Several wallets

All transactions are built and signed in the browser. This site's server relays requests to Helius so the API key stays private, keeps the public token listing, stores artwork, and runs the desk. It never receives a key or a passphrase.

Zero-knowledge proofs for private transfers are produced by Helius's prover, which runs inside an attested enclave. The wallet verifies the enclave's signed policy and encrypts each request to the enclave's key before sending it, so this site's relay carries only ciphertext. It follows that the secrecy of a transfer's contents relies on that enclave as well as on the cryptography on chain.

External wallets are supported through the Wallet Standard as a beta. The wallet signs one message from which its privacy keys are derived, and it must be able to sign version 1 transactions.

Risks

Read these.

  • Tokens here are experimentalAnyone can launch one. Most will go to zero. Nothing on this site is advice.
  • Privacy is partialWallets are public. Pool trades, deposits, withdrawals and claims are public. Amount and timing can link a deposit to the trade that follows it.
  • Pool trades can be sandwichedA Shielded or public trade is an ordinary swap that anyone can see coming. Bots can trade around it and cost you up to your slippage limit. Keep the limit as tight as the token's liquidity allows. A Ghost fill does not touch the pool and cannot be sandwiched.
  • New softwareSolana Privacy Rings is new, its developer kit is pre-release, and this site's own code has not been audited. Use amounts you can afford to lose.
  • Programs this site does not controlMeteora's and Solana Privacy Rings' programs are run and can be upgraded by their own teams. The fee guarantees above describe what the creator and this platform can and cannot do.
  • The desk is a counterpartyIt cannot take your funds, but it learns your trades, it can run out of inventory, and it can be switched off. The Shielded route does not depend on it.
  • Browser storageA ghost wallet lives in this browser. Clearing site data deletes it. Export the key and keep it somewhere safe.
  • Independent projectNot affiliated with Helius, Meteora, the Solana Foundation or the Privacy Cash protocol at privacycash.org.
