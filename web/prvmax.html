One wallet. Ten private balances.

A vault is a wallet of its own with its own private balance, made from your wallet's key. Spread SOL or a token across your vaults, trade from them, see everything in one place, and call it all back to one wallet. What each vault holds, and what moves between you and a vault, is encrypted. The vaults themselves are public, and so is every trade on a pool.

What a vault is

An ordinary Solana keypair with its own private balance in Solana Privacy Rings. Its key is worked out from your wallet's key, in this browser, so there is nothing new to back up, and you can export any vault's key and use it in any wallet.

Do

Ready, spread, trade, recall.

Each step runs one vault after another and says what it is doing. If a step stops, what already settled stays settled: the table above is read again, so you can see where things stand before pressing anything twice.

Before a vault can be used it needs two things: some public SOL of its own, because network fees are paid from public SOL, and a one-time registration with Rings, so that it can be sent to privately. Your wallet sends the SOL in one transaction; each vault registers itself.

Splits an amount from your wallet's private balance evenly across the vaults you tick, as private transfers. The amount is taken from what your wallet holds privately: to spread public SOL or public tokens, deposit them on the Wallet page first.

Into these vaults

Buys one token from each vault you tick, paid from that vault's private balance. The vaults buy one after another, so each buy moves the price the next one pays.

From these vaults

Sells a share of one token from each vault you tick. What a sale brings in stays in that vault's private balance, in the token's pair token.

From these vaults

Empties every vault into one wallet: all ten are checked, whether or not they are in use. Each vault's private balances go first, then anything a trade left in its ordinary token accounts, then its public SOL, down to zero.

Every vault is an ordinary Solana keypair. Its secret key works in any Solana wallet, and on this site's Wallet page under Import a secret key, where the vault joins your list as a wallet of its own. That is the way to reach a vault without this page. Anyone who has a vault's secret key controls what that vault holds.
