Why a built-in wallet

Rings transactions are version 1 transactions and need a viewing key next to the signing key. The ghost wallet handles both without waiting for extension support, and it is a plain Solana keypair: export it and import it anywhere.

Read-only access

Viewing pass

Lets someone read this wallet's private balance and history and do nothing else: an accountant, an auditor, or you on another device. A pass cannot send, unshield, trade or sign. What a pass holds

Before you make one
  • Its holder sees everything this wallet does privately, from its first day and for as long as it is used: tokens, amounts and the wallets on the other side.
  • It cannot be taken back. This wallet's keys never change, so the only way to end a pass is to move to another wallet.
  • It is sealed with a passphrase of its own. Send the pass one way and the passphrase another.
