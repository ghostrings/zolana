## Checking a token

On any token's page, **Token facts** lists its mint, its curve pool, its graduated pool once it has one, and its creator, each linked to the explorer. It also shows the supply now, what has been burned, and whether more can be minted, all read from chain.

## Who controls these programs

Solana Privacy Rings and Meteora are run, and can be upgraded, by their own teams. This platform controls neither. The guarantees on these pages describe what the creator and this platform can and cannot do.
